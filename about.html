ABOUT CHESSMOVELAB

Improve Your Chess Through Better Analysis

ChessMoveLab is a free online chess analysis tool designed to help players understand their games and learn from every move.

Players can review recent games, explore move-by-move analysis, examine engine evaluations, identify mistakes and blunders, discover strong alternatives and better understand their openings.

Our Goal

Our goal is simple: make useful chess analysis easy to access, easy to understand and available to players who want to improve their game.

Chess Engine Analysis

ChessMoveLab uses the Stockfish chess engine to help evaluate positions and suggest strong moves. Engine evaluations are intended as learning tools and may vary depending on analysis depth and position complexity.

Game Information

When supported, ChessMoveLab can retrieve publicly available game information so players can review their own recent chess games in a convenient analysis interface.

Independent project

ChessMoveLab is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Chess.com.
