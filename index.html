ABOUT CHESSMOVELAB

What is ChessMoveLab?

ChessMoveLab is a free chess game analysis tool for players who want to understand what happened in their games instead of simply looking at the final result.

Enter your Chess.com username, choose one of your recent public games, and review the moves on an interactive board. ChessMoveLab uses Stockfish analysis to help you examine positions, compare moves, and identify important moments from the game.

The goal is to make post-game review simple and practical. You can move through a game step by step, inspect how the position changes, and spend more time on the moments that actually matter.

HOW IT WORKS

Review your games step by step

ChessMoveLab retrieves available public game information from Chess.com after you enter a username. You can then select a recent game and open it in the analysis board.

The board recreates the game move by move. Stockfish evaluates positions and can help you compare the move that was played with stronger alternatives.

You can use the navigation controls to move backward and forward through the game at your own pace. No ChessMoveLab account is required to review supported public games.

LEARN FROM YOUR GAMES

Why analyze your chess games?

Playing more games gives you experience, but reviewing those games helps you understand where your decisions can improve.

A single game can reveal an opening problem, a missed tactical opportunity, an unsafe king, a weak pawn structure, or an endgame that could have been handled differently.

When reviewing a game, useful questions include:

  • Where did the position start to become difficult?
  • Was there a stronger move available?
  • Did I miss a tactical idea?
  • Was my opening position comfortable?
  • Which part of the game should I study next?

STOCKFISH ANALYSIS

Understanding engine suggestions

Stockfish is a powerful chess engine that evaluates positions and calculates strong continuations. An engine score is useful, but the most important part of analysis is understanding why the position changed.

When Stockfish recommends a different move, compare that move with the move played in the game. Look for checks, captures, threats, piece activity, king safety, and changes in the pawn structure.

You do not need to memorize every engine move. The purpose of reviewing a game is to recognize ideas and patterns that can help you make better decisions in future games.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ChessMoveLab free?

Yes. ChessMoveLab can be used to review supported public Chess.com games without creating a ChessMoveLab account.

Do I need my Chess.com password?

No. Never enter your Chess.com password. ChessMoveLab only needs a public username to retrieve available public game information.

Can beginners use ChessMoveLab?

Yes. Beginners can review games move by move and focus on mistakes, tactical opportunities, and stronger alternatives.

Does ChessMoveLab replace a chess coach?

No. Engine analysis is useful for evaluating positions and finding strong moves, while a coach can provide personalized explanations and training plans.

Why can the best engine move be difficult to understand?

Chess engines calculate variations many moves ahead. Sometimes the purpose of a move only becomes clear later in the variation, so it can help to explore the suggested continuation instead of looking only at the first move.
