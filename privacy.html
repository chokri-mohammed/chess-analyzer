PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ChessMoveLab may collect, use and process information when you use our website.

1. Information You Provide

ChessMoveLab allows you to enter a public Chess.com username in order to retrieve and analyze publicly available chess games.

ChessMoveLab does not ask for your Chess.com password, payment information or Chess.com login credentials.

2. Public Chess Game Data

When you search for a Chess.com username, ChessMoveLab may retrieve publicly available game information associated with that username.

This information may include:

  • Player usernames
  • Chess ratings
  • Game results
  • Move history
  • Game dates
  • Other publicly available game information

3. Browser Storage

ChessMoveLab may temporarily store certain information in your browser, such as selected game information or navigation data, so that analysis features work correctly.

This browser storage remains on your device unless it is removed by the website or cleared through your browser.

4. Google Analytics

ChessMoveLab uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and to help us improve the service.

Google Analytics may collect information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Approximate location
  • Device and browser information
  • Visit duration
  • Traffic source
  • Interactions with the website

Google may process this information according to its own privacy policies.

You can learn more at Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Third-party services used by ChessMoveLab, including analytics services, may use cookies or similar technologies to measure website activity and improve their services.

6. Email Communications

If you contact ChessMoveLab by email, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

This information is used only to respond to your message, provide support or review feedback.

7. Third-Party Services

ChessMoveLab may depend on third-party services to provide parts of its functionality, including chess game data, analytics and website hosting.

These third parties operate according to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the website and the information processed through it. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

ChessMoveLab is not designed to intentionally collect sensitive personal information from children.

Users should avoid submitting personal or sensitive information through the website.
